Fetchora

Download manager for files, YouTube and other video sites, and torrents. Free, and no account needed.

🍎 Mac Your device

macOS 14 or newer · Apple Silicon or Intel

Download for Mac (.dmg)
  1. Open the DMG and drag Fetchora onto Applications.
  2. Open Fetchora. When macOS says it can't be opened, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway and enter your password.
  4. For YouTube and torrents, press Install in the banner at the top. This needs Homebrew.

🪟 Windows Your device

Windows 10 or 11 · 64-bit

Download for Windows (.exe)
  1. Put Fetchora.exe in a folder you'll keep. There's no installer.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  3. For YouTube and torrents, press Install in the blue banner at the top.

🐧 Ubuntu Your device

Ubuntu 22.04 or newer · 64-bit (Intel/AMD)

Download for Ubuntu (.deb)
  1. Download Fetchora.deb to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open Terminal (Ctrl+Alt+T) and run:
    sudo apt install ~/Downloads/Fetchora.deb
  3. Open Fetchora from Show Apps.
  4. For YouTube, press Install in the blue banner at the top.

To uninstall: sudo apt remove fetchora

🤖 Android Your device

Android 8 or newer

Download for Android (.apk)

Older or 32-bit phone? Get this version instead

  1. Open the downloaded Fetchora.apk. If asked, let your browser install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  2. Share any video or link from YouTube, Chrome or Instagram to Fetchora, then pick a quality.
  3. Files are saved in Download/Fetchora.
